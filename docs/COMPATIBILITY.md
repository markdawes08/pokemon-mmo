### COMP-028: Bounded shared development exploration

ADR-012 enables only `r1-exploration-v1` for the source-pinned `r1-squirtle-v1` local fixture. Logical map groups reuse one private character connection and owner, with nonblocking public avatars. Static initial NPCs still block tiles. Running is permitted by this development policy without granting real story unlocks; walking remains default and Shift runs outdoors. Three known Pallet coordinate story triggers are bypassed and random encounters are disabled. Ordinary staged trainers cannot enter.

Twenty-Hz simulation and ten-Hz periodic publication plus immediate acknowledgements are the initial local implementation. Source step/ledge timing is enforced; committed transfers precede client door presentation, adding presentation latency. Prediction, full private story overlays, separate routable zone rooms, sixty-second transport reconnection grace and broader latency/load gates are not implemented. Recovery uses fresh authenticated admission and the last durable checkpoint. External session revocation is detected by the next input/checkpoint or heartbeat, then removes presence.

Full gate: 2026-09-26T01:28:07.942Z; 27 world storage groups, 18 network groups and four world browser scenarios within all 35 passing browser cases. reports/world-rendering.json documents bounded ordered-message-delay sampling, not a population or network-partition benchmark. Normal accounts remain unseeded by setup/tests; no public deployment occurs.
